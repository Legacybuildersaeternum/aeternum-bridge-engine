Developer Note - Future Family Reconnection Request (Scaffold Only)
Potential relative discovery should create a reconnection request, never an automatic merge. Future matching can use safe fields such as family name, birth-year range, region/state/country, relationship clues, and contact preference. Before any merge or persistent lineage link is accepted, both parties must first confirm outside contact (phone, text, email, or mail).
